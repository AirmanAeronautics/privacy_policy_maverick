TOGA ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by Airman Aeronautics and provides a pilot learning and study platform designed to support aviation theory training and exam preparation.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, and share personal data when you use our services. This policy applies to our mobile applications and web platforms. By using TOGA, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

01

Roles & Data Control

Platform Roles

TOGA is designed for individual users across the aviation training ecosystem:

  • Cadets — student pilots preparing for theory examinations
  • Instructors — certified flight instructors supporting learner progress
  • Seasoned Pilots — experienced aviators using the platform for reference and refresher training
Data Controller

Airman Aeronautics is the primary data controller responsible for handling your personal data in accordance with applicable privacy regulations.

02

Information We Collect

Identity Information
Name & email address
Phone number
Profile photo
Country & city
Study & Learning Data
  • Quiz performance and scores
  • Progress tracking across modules
  • Learning activity and engagement signals
  • Achievements and badges earned
Device & Technical Data
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • Usage analytics and interaction patterns
Permissions Data

Depending on your device settings, we may request access to:

  • Storage / file access (for uploads, if applicable)
  • Camera (for profile photo, if applicable)
  • Device information
03

Use of Data

We process your data to deliver and improve the TOGA platform:

  • Provide study and learning features
  • Track academic progress and performance
  • Personalise the learning experience for your role and goals
  • Improve platform performance, reliability, and features
  • Generate AI-powered study insights through Airman AI
04

Artificial Intelligence (Airman AI)

TOGA uses Airman AI to enhance the learning experience, including study recommendations, performance insights, and learning assistance.

AI Processing
  • AI outputs may be stored and associated with your account
  • User data may be used to improve AI model accuracy over time
  • AI processing may involve trusted third-party AI providers
⚠ Critical — AI Limitations

Airman AI is advisory only. AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate or incomplete. AI does not replace official study materials, formal training, or professional guidance. Users must independently verify important information before relying on it for any aviation-related decision.

05

Data Sharing

Service Providers

We may share data with trusted third-party providers who help us operate the platform, including cloud infrastructure providers (e.g. AWS) and analytics providers (e.g. Firebase, Mixpanel). These providers are bound by data processing agreements and may only use data for the purposes we specify.

Legal Disclosure

We may disclose your data when required by law, regulatory authority, or valid legal process.

We do not sell your data  ·  We do not use your data for advertising
06

Data Retention

  • Data is retained for as long as your account remains active
  • Upon account deletion, your data is retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion
  • Certain data may be retained longer where required by law or compliance obligations
07

Your Rights

You have rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of the following, contact us at hello@theairman.org.

Access
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Correction
Correct or update inaccurate personal information.
Deletion
Delete your account and associated personal data.
Export
Receive a portable copy of your data in a usable format.
08

Children & Age Policy

TOGA is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided data to us, please contact hello@theairman.org and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

09

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Secure cloud infrastructure with restricted access controls
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Regular monitoring and security reviews

No system is completely secure. While we take the protection of your data seriously, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10

AI & Learning Disclaimer

TOGA is a learning support platform. It does not replace:

  • Formal aviation training programmes
  • Professional instructor-led instruction
  • Official approved study materials and examination resources
Important

All learning decisions remain the responsibility of the user. TOGA is a study aid — not a regulatory or operational authority.

11

Limitation of Liability

Airman Aeronautics shall not be liable for:

  • Learning outcomes or examination performance
  • Decisions made based on AI-generated study insights
  • Any actions taken in reliance on content provided through the platform
12

Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as TOGA evolves. Any changes will be published with an updated effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform following a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
