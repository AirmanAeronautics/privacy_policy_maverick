03

How We Use Your Data

We process personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Core app functionality — account management, authentication, app features
  • Flight training workflows — FTO system, sortie management, evaluations
  • Instructor–cadet interaction — sharing feedback, grading, scheduling
  • Regulatory compliance — maintaining aviation authority–required records
  • AI-driven insights — logbook autofill, mission briefings, training recommendations via Airman AI
  • Service improvement — analytics, diagnostics, performance monitoring

No Advertising. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to any third party for commercial purposes.

06

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Upon account deletion:

  • Your data is retained for a 30-day grace period to allow recovery.
  • After 30 days, your data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems.
  • Certain data may be retained longer if required by aviation regulatory obligations.

Users may request data export (e.g., logbook) at any time via the app or by contacting us.

07

Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational security measures to protect your data:

  • Infrastructure: Hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) with enterprise-grade security controls
  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Access Control: Role-based access — instructors access only assigned cadet data; admins access only organisation-scoped data
  • Authentication: Secure session management and credential handling

Despite these measures, no system is completely immune to security threats. We encourage users to use strong passwords and report any suspected security issues to hello@theairman.org.

09

Children & Age Policy

TOGA is available to users aged 13 and above. Users under 18 are permitted only under:

  • Verified flight school supervision
  • Explicit instructor oversight

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately for deletion.
